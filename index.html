Welcome to Decimal Muncher!

Use the arrow keys or WASD to move through the maze. Collect yellow dots for points, catch a glowing ? to compare decimals, and dodge the ghosts! Choose the greater or smaller number as directed.

3 levels • tenths, hundredths & thousandths • no login needed

How to play: Collect dots (+10), grab question markers (?) and choose the correct decimal (+100). Wrong answers cost a life. Ghosts also cost a life when they catch you. Finish 5 comparisons to advance to the next level. Press 1 or 2 to answer a question, or click a choice. Press P to pause. Tip: A decimal with more digits is not necessarily greater; compare place values.
